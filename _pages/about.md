I am now an Algorithm Engineer at the Qwen Large Model Application Team, Alibaba. Prior to joining Alibaba, I received both my M.S. and B.S. degrees in Computer Science and Technology from Zhejiang University. During my master's studies, I was a member of [ZJUNLP](https://github.com/zjunlp), advised by Professor [Huajun Chen (陈华钧)](https://person.zju.edu.cn/huajun) and Professor [Ningyu Zhang (张宁豫)](https://person.zju.edu.cn/ningyu).

My current research interests focus on Large Language Models (LLMs) and their theory and applications. I'm interested in the Mechanism Interpretability of LLMs and the application of LLMs to Autonomous Agents. I have published several papers <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=QVTr5dQAAAAJ'><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Citations-{{ site.data.citations.author.citedby }}-4285F4?logo=googlescholar&labelColor=f6f6f6&style=flat"></a> at the top international NLP conferences such as ACL and NAACL.
